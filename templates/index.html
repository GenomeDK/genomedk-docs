High-performance computing for all

GenomeDK is a high-performance computing infrastructure for research and other compute-intensive work. We provide computing power, storage, and support to researchers, students, public institutions, and small and medium-sized companies.

All wrapped up in an ISO 27001-certified, mature, and flexible platform located in Aarhus, Denmark.

Request account

Organize and collaborate with project folders

Organize and control access to your data in project folders, to help you reach GDPR compliance.

A safe home for your sensitive data

We take information security seriously. GenomeDK is ISO 27001 certified with yearly ISAE 3000 declarations.

Supports you at every step

We provide up-to-date documentation, friendly support, and workshops so that you can get the help you need and concentrate on the science.

Bring your own software

Use Conda or Pixi to install and manage the software you need, or build it from scratch. It's up to you!

Explore data interactively

With the GenomeDK Desktop, you can access your code and data right from your browser. Analyze your data interactively in e.g. RStudio or MATLAB, with dedicated resources for your session.

Stay in control with a closed zone

Large research projects with strict data control requirements can request a closed zone to be able to control data leaving the zone.
